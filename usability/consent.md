# Consent note

Read this to each participant before the session, and record their answer.

You are invited to try Botwork, a language for automating tasks and acceptance
tests, in a session of about 90 minutes. We are testing the language and its
documentation, not you. If a task is hard, that tells us what to improve.

- You will work through six short tasks using only the published
  documentation. We will time each task and note where you get stuck. A task
  ends when you finish, when you choose to stop, or after 20 minutes.
- The facilitator will not help with the tasks. You may ask about the setup at
  any time.
- We record your task results, times, and comments under a code such as P07,
  together with whether you are new to test automation. We do not record your
  name with the results, audio, video, or your screen.
- Pseudonymous results may be published in the project's usability report.
- Taking part is voluntary. You may stop at any time, and you may ask for your
  results to be removed until they are published.

Do you agree to take part? Record: participant code, date, and "agreed".
